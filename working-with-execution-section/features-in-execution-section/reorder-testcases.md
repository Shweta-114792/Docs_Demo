# Reorder Testcases

Below are the steps to Reorder Testcases in the Execution List:

1. On the **Execution** tab, navigate to the Testcase. Hover the cursor over the **Order History** Testcas&#x65;**.** A drag and drop Icon (Hand Icon) appears.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (4365).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Drag the **Order History** Testcase and drop it after the **Fetch Product Details** Testcase in 2nd position to reorder it.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (4366).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
