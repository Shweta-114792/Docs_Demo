# Reports

Use this section to view common questions related to reports and analysis. It includes queries about generating reports, interpreting results, analyzing test outcomes, analyzing failures, and monitor the overall status of test runs.

* **Do reports provide step-level execution details?**\
  **Ans**: Yes. Reports include step-level information that shows the status of every action executed in a test case. Each step displays whether it passes, fails, or is skipped.
*   **Are execution results available in the Reports tab?**

    **Ans**: Yes. The **Reports** displays the results of executed test cases and shows whether each test case passes or fails after execution.
*   **Are reports automatically generated before test execution?**

    **Ans**: No. Reports are generated only after test case execution completes. The system records the results during execution and then makes them available in the [Reports](../../../create-and-execute-tests-with-design-studio/working-with-reports-section/).
