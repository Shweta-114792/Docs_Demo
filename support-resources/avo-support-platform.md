# Avo Support Platform

The Avo Support Portal provides a centralized location for accessing support-related services. Use the AI-powered chatbot to get quick answers to your questions. You can also explore additional support options available within the portal whenever you need assistance.

## **Getting Started with the Avo Support Portal**

1. Visit [Avo Support Portal](https://support.avoautomation.com/home) and explore its features like the Knowledge Base collection and the **Search Help?** widget. Find Avo Assure documentation, how-to guides, troubleshooting information, and knowledge base articles per your need.
2. Click **Raise a Request** to navigate to the Tickets page. (Registration required for first time users, please register with your company email id only)

**Tip**: Bookmark the portal for quick access.
