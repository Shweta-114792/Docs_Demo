# Login to Avo Assure with SSO

### SSO (Single Sign-On)&#x20;

SSO simplifies user authentication across multiple systems, and RBAC ensures users can only access resources appropriate to their roles. Together, they provide secure and efficient access management.&#x20;

### Steps to Login&#x20;

1. Click Use Single Sign-On/SAML

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (362).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Select the account to login

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (363).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

User will be logged in to Avo Assure, and if the user is assigned as Admin, they will be navigated directly to the Admin tab, where they can manage verticals and map the users to projects by navigating to Vertical Management section.
